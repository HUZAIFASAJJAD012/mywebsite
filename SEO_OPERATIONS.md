# SEO and lead-generation operations

The codebase provides the technical foundation. Search visibility and lead quality should be improved with verified production data, not fabricated claims or bulk-generated pages.

## Production launch

1. Set the exact production values for `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_LINKEDIN_URL`, `NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_MEASUREMENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION`, `BING_SITE_VERIFICATION` and `SITE_LAST_MODIFIED` in Vercel.
2. Confirm SMTP or the contact webhook delivers a real test enquiry before advertising the form.
3. Verify the domain property in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
4. Submit `https://www.huzaifasajjaddev.online/sitemap.xml` once.
5. Inspect and request indexing for the homepage, About page, Services index, core service pages and strongest case studies. Do not repeatedly request indexing for minor edits.
6. Test homepage and project social previews after deployment.

## Monthly review

Record Google Search Console impressions, clicks, CTR, average position and qualified enquiries for:

- Brand: `Huzaifa Sajjad`, `Huzaifa Sajjad developer`, `Huzaifa Sajjad full stack developer`
- Commercial: `freelance full stack developer`, `hire full stack developer`, `Next.js developer`, `web developer`, `small business website developer`, `mobile app developer`

Review results by page, country and device. Improve pages when real queries reveal missing information or weak click-through rates. Measure success primarily through qualified enquiries, with WhatsApp, email, LinkedIn and project engagement as secondary conversions.
